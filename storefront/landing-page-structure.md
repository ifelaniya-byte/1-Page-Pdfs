# Storefront landing structure

## 1. Hero section
- Headline: "One-page systems for the stress points people feel every day"
- Subheadline: "AI overload, money stress, caregiving, home chaos, and business instability"
- CTA: "Shop top-selling bundles"

## 2. Best sellers row
- AI Burnout Recovery Pack
- Money Stress Survival Pack
- Caregiver Relief Bundle
- Senior Care Family Bundle
- Small Business Cash Flow Bundle

## 3. Best niche collections
- Healthcare & Insurance Pack
- Home Ops Reset Pack
- Digital Wellness Starter Pack
- Pet Care Essentials Bundle
- Home Services & Risk Pack

## 4. Why buyers buy
- One-page clarity
- Immediate use
- Clear problem-to-solution match
- Low-friction purchase
- Premium but still affordable

## 5. Bundle merchandising
- Each bundle should show:
  - pain point
  - audience
  - included products
  - value price
  - short promise statement

## 6. Long-tail archive
- Full catalog remains in the repo and is not the main storefront.
- Use it for SEO, product research, and expansion after front-end conversion wins.

## 7. Sales rule
- Lead with the pain, not the catalog breadth.
- Sell the relief first; sell the document second.
